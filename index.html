    AN ARTIST OVER TWO DECADES

    DRAKE

    An exploration of the albums, eras, and evolution of one of the most influential artists of his generation.

    Aubrey Drake Graham emerged from Toronto in the late 2000s and, over a run of mixtapes and albums, rewired what a rap star was allowed to sound like — part confessional singer, part hitmaker, part internet-native provocateur. His catalog moves between moods as much as genres: the wounded soul of Take Care, the arena-sized bravado of Views, the loose, single-driven sprawl of his 2020s run.

    This site traces that catalog project by project, from the early mixtape years that built his name to the sprawling, collaborative present. Use the wheel on the left to move through the timeline — scroll, click, or use the arrow keys to bring a project to the center and its story will appear here.
